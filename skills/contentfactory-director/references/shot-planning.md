# Plan shots for motion and continuity

Keep the screenwriter's beats in order. For each essential shot, record approximate range, the viewer's new information, visible action from start through result, framing and blocking, continuity into/out of the shot, sound cue, source route and acceptance evidence. Put these notes in supported creative-task fields alongside reference pins. Planning labels have no platform identity until a timeline is saved.

- Build movement from a cause and observable result. In a demonstration, show input, operation and output. In performance, give the actor a playable action and let the response read. In an explainer, move the object or relationship doing the explaining. Quiet action may use a locked shot. Framing movement should reveal genuinely new information.
- Design adjoining shots together. Record the exiting pose, prop state, gaze, screen direction and sound tail, then match them at entry or motivate a deliberate discontinuity. A cut can carry a gesture, sound or matched object across a seam. Inspect both sides at the exact timestamps. Prefer a clean cut when the effect would hide missing action.
- Make a visual decision that fits the work. Anchor palette, contrast, lighting, type and framing to a reviewed frame or source. Reserve the focal area for the subject. Check text size and crop in the delivery aspect ratio. Limit decoration that competes with proof, the beat or the user's brand. Sound marks an actual event or supports a deliberate rhythm.
- Show viewable inspected frames and proposed object/character placement before generated motion. Use the [approval procedure](../../contentfactory/references/director-approval.md). Reuse approved pins and decide how each is bound with the [generation specialist](../../contentfactory-generate/references/video.md).
- After approval, test the hardest essential interaction early. Before full delivery, inspect a timed assembly, repair the specific failed interval, and check the exact final export. A contact sheet can diagnose continuity, cropping or identity. Playback is needed for movement, timing and joins. The [final audit](../../contentfactory/references/quality-audit.md) covers the entire finished video.

Example shot card: **Refill, about 0:06–0:11.** In profile, a runner places the glass bottle under the spout. The frame holds the bottle center and the hand on its right edge. Show the stream and rising water line before the hand lifts the bottle. Carry the water sound into the next shot and preserve label orientation, grip and screen direction. Use the pinned bottle reference and approved blocking. Accept when playback shows the water rise and the full lift without a shape change or accidental freeze.
