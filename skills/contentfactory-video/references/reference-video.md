# Reference Video Adaptation

Use this workflow for reproducing/adapting supplied clips or comparing a cut
against a reference. Ordinary background footage follows `preparation.md`.
Recover an owned copy of the selected source before importing another one.

For YouTube, discover native `research_collect.youtubeDownload`, preflight
collection within the existing budget and use a stable idempotency key.
`youtubeVideos` is metadata, not playable media. Poll its operation and retain
ordered `content_operation_result_get.research.media` asset/file references.
For another URL type, discover the supported ContentFactory import path.
Local files require the user's ContentFactory upload. Do not use external search,
downloaders, browsers or shell tools to bypass an unavailable import.

Probe the actual owned file and retain source URL, interval, asset/file IDs and
receipt. Collection can cost money; a URL does not authorize unlimited spending.
A download link or successful metadata lookup does not establish viewing.

## Fidelity and Evidence

Separate required action sequence, timing, camera/staging, style, characters
and audio. Preserve explicit choices. If "copy" leaves a consequential choice
between action/timing fidelity and style borrowing, resolve it before dependent
paid generation. Continue authorized collection/inspection where possible.

Record intervals actually watched, timestamped frames, model-assisted coverage
and listening as separate evidence. ContentFactory native previews and analysis
may help; creating a clip is not watching it. Discover `video_analyze`,
`media_inspect`, `video_review` and compatible models before declaring review
unavailable. Use only matching preflight and an authorized review ceiling.

Model analysis, a transcript, contact sheet or first/last stills cannot prove
continuous playback, exact choreography or lip sync. When playback or analysis
is unavailable, mark affected properties and intervals unverified and resolve
material unknown actions before spending on scenes that depend on them.

Use one compact row per action beat:

| Source time | Observed action/state change | Camera/staging | Output time/scene | Evidence/uncertainty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Start-end | Actor, direction, prop, start/end state | Framing and movement | Required duration and beat | Viewed interval, frame times or review receipt |

Cover the selected interval and mark gaps instead of inventing missing action.
Preserve required causal order, entrances/exits, geography, prop/door states and
timing. Separate observed facts from creative additions. Carry the beat plan
into screenplay and accepted scene references. Split only where supported
generation limits require it while retaining the requested runtime and action.

## Compare the Export

Compare at matching source/output times using the agreed fidelity. Check joins
and action changes, not only the opening style. Correct material departures
within authorization and recheck affected intervals. Preserve required frame,
final-video and audio checks in their focused references. Report matches,
intentional differences, unresolved differences and actual review coverage.
